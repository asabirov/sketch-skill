# Channel thread panel

Captured: 2026-11-24, recorded by the owner
Best for: a team chat with busy channels, where a person sees which message has a thread, reads its last reply without leaving the channel, and reads and answers the thread while the channel keeps its place.
Row opens: in a pane. The thread of a card opens in a pane at the right of the channel. In a window below 1000 px, the pane takes the place of the channel.
Related: `chat/conversation-pane` shows a list of conversations with the open conversation next to it. `content/comment-thread` shows the replies in place under a comment, with no pane. `navigation/topics-tree-side-pane` lists the topics of a channel in the navigation. `notifications/follow-menu` is the notification choice that the bell of a thread opens. `navigation/swipe-back` is the drag that the phone layout reuses: a drag to the right from the left edge takes the thread away.

## Transitions

The pictures show no motion. Each value below is the designer's choice, so each value is estimated. The values of the drag are the measured values of `navigation/swipe-back`. With reduced motion set in the system, each change is immediate.

- `channel` to `thread-open`. Trigger: click a card or its link, or press Enter on the link. Moves: the panel goes from clear to solid, animation panel_in 150 ms, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1). The channel gets narrow at once. estimated.
- `channel` to `thread-empty`. Trigger: click the card of a thread with no replies. Moves: the panel goes from clear to solid, animation panel_in 150 ms, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1). estimated.
- `thread-open` or `thread-empty` to `reply-sent`. Trigger: click the send button of the panel, or press Enter in its message field. Moves: the new message goes from clear to solid, animation message_in 150 ms, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1). The card in the channel changes at once. estimated.
- `thread-open`, `thread-empty` or `reply-sent` to `thread-closed`. Trigger: click the close button, or press Escape. Moves: the panel goes at once, and the channel gets its width back at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `thread-open` with a new width of the panel. Trigger: drag the handle, or press the Left or the Right arrow key on it. Moves: the line follows the pointer, and the channel takes the other space. A key moves the line 16 px at once. This move has no duration, so `transitions.json` has no entry for it. Each other state with an open panel has the same handle.
- `thread-open` to the thread of a different card. Trigger: click a different card while the panel is open. Moves: the panel shows the other thread at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `narrow-channel` to `narrow-thread`. Trigger: click a card in a window below 1000 px. Moves: the thread goes from clear to solid, animation panel_in 150 ms, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1). estimated. The capture script records a window of 1280 px. The agent recorded this entry and the entries of the drag in a window of 390 px.
- `narrow-thread` to `narrow-channel`. Trigger: click the Back button, or press Escape. Moves: the channel shows again at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `narrow-thread` to `narrow-dragging`. Trigger: a drag to the right from the left edge, in a window below 600 px. Moves: the thread, by the distance of the finger. The channel moves from 48 px left to its position, and its dim goes from full to none, in step with the thread. This move has no duration. Each other state with an open thread starts the drag in the same way.
- `narrow-dragging` to `narrow-channel`. Trigger: a release after one third of the screen width. A flick also goes back, if it is longer than 24 px and faster than 0.5 px in a millisecond. Moves: the `transform` of the thread to the right edge, the `transform` of the channel to its position, and the `opacity` of the dim to 0. 520 ms, the `linear()` spring of `navigation/swipe-back`. estimated.
- `narrow-dragging` to `narrow-thread`. Trigger: a release before one third of the screen width. Moves: the same 3 properties, back to the start. 520 ms, the same `linear()` spring. estimated.
- `channel` to `card-unread`. Trigger: a reply from a different person comes while the thread is closed. Moves: the mark and the newest reply show on the card at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `card-unread` to `thread-open`. Trigger: click the card or its link. Moves: the panel shows as in `channel` to `thread-open`. The mark of the card goes at once. estimated.
- `channel` to `thread-loading`. Trigger: click a card while the replies are not on the device. Moves: the panel shows as in `channel` to `thread-open`, with the note in the place of the replies. estimated.
- `thread-loading` to `thread-open` or `thread-error`. Trigger: the replies come, or the load fails. Moves: the replies or the error take the place of the note at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `thread-error` to `thread-loading`. Trigger: click Retry. Moves: the note takes the place of the error at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `thread-open` to `reply-failed`. Trigger: the send of a reply fails. Moves: the error line and the 2 buttons show under the reply at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `reply-failed` to `reply-sent` or `thread-open`. Trigger: click Retry, or click Delete. Moves: the error line goes at once. After Delete, the reply goes also. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `thread-open` to `more-menu` or `notify-menu`. Trigger: click the more button, or click the bell. Moves: the menu or the box shows at once. An action, Escape or a press outside closes it at once, and the focus goes back to the button. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `more-menu` to `thread-archived`. Trigger: click "Archive thread". Moves: the note shows above the message field at once, and the card changes at once. 0 ms, none. estimated.
- `thread-archived` to `reply-sent` or `thread-open`. Trigger: send a message, or click "Open thread again" in the more menu. Moves: the note goes at once. A new message shows as in `thread-open` to `reply-sent`. estimated.
- `quote-deleted`, `thread-locked` and `channel-empty` show with the page. They have no transition.

## Hard cases

- The place in the channel: when the panel opens or closes, the words of the channel wrap again. A channel that shows its newest message stays at its newest message. In each other position, the card of the thread stays at the same height on the screen. This is drawn. The pictures show one position only.
- The place in the thread: each time that a thread opens, the panel shows its newest reply. The size of the window, the width of the panel and the layout can change while the thread is closed. The thread still opens at its newest reply. Drawn.
- Words in a message field: the words that the person typed in the channel field stay while the thread is open and after it closes. The words in the thread field stay when the same thread closes and opens again. Drawn.
- Long thread name: in the card, the name goes on to a second line, and the link goes below it. In the bar of the panel and in the placeholder, the name is cut with an ellipsis.
- Long last reply: the reply stays on one line and is cut with an ellipsis. The age stays in view at the end of the line.
- Long quoted message: the quote stays on one line and is cut with an ellipsis. Seen in the source.
- A narrow channel next to the panel, in a window of about 1000 px: the channel is about 440 px wide. The note of a card goes on to a second line, and the line to the card still ends at the middle of the card.
- Many cards in view: each card sits under its own message, and each has its own line from the avatar. Seen in the source with 3 cards.
- Keyboard: the Tab key reaches the link of each card in the order of the messages. Enter opens the thread. Escape closes the thread, and the focus goes back to the link of the card. In a narrow window, the Back button does the work of the drag. Drawn.
- A wide window, such as 1920 px: the panel starts 480 px wide, and the channel takes the other space. The words of a message go at most 72 characters wide.
- The width of the panel: the panel starts at 32% of the window width, from 340 px to 480 px. The handle stops where the panel is 300 px wide, and where the channel is 360 px wide. Home and End on the handle go to these 2 limits. A double click on the handle, or Enter, gives the start width. Drawn.
- The width after a change: the browser keeps the width, and each thread opens with it on the next visit. When the window gets narrow, the panel gets narrow first, down to 300 px. Its kept width comes back when the window gets wide again. Drawn.
- The handle in a narrow window: below 1000 px there is no handle, because the thread has the full width of the channel. Drawn.
- The handle for a keyboard and a screen reader: the Tab key reaches the handle after the channel. The handle is a separator with the name "Width of the thread panel". It gives the width of the panel in px, with its smallest and its largest value. Drawn.
- Empty: `channel-empty` is a channel with no messages, and `thread-empty` is a thread with no replies. The source shows the empty thread only as a card.
- Slow network: the panel opens at once with the message of the channel, which is on the device, and the note of `thread-loading`. Drawn.
- Error: `thread-error` is a load that fails, and `reply-failed` is a send that fails. Each has a Retry button. Drawn.
- A reply that was not sent: the reply and its error line stay when the panel closes and opens again. They go only after Retry or Delete, so the person does not lose the words. Drawn.
- A quote of a message with no words, such as a spoken message: the source shows a short note in the place of the words. The sketch has no such message.
- Drag that starts outside the edge: a drag that starts more than 24 px from the left edge does not move the thread. A drag up or down scrolls the thread. Drawn.
- Stopped gesture: if the system stops the gesture, the thread goes back to its position. Drawn.
- Drag from 600 px: there is no drag. The navigation is at the left of the thread, so the left edge of the thread is not the edge of the screen. Drawn.
- Reduced motion: the thread still follows the finger. After the release, the end position shows at once. Drawn.

## Notes

- The card has 3 forms. The channel of the sketch shows one card of each form, as the source shows 2 forms in one channel.
  - Form `last-reply`: the link "See thread", and below it the name of the writer, the last reply and its age. Use it when the thread has a reply from the last days, so that the person reads the news without opening the thread. State `channel`, and state `reply-sent` after a new reply. The pictures show no card with a reply, so this form is drawn.
  - Form `count`: the count of the messages, such as "2 messages", is the link. Below it are the note "There are no recent messages in this thread." and the age of the last message. This is a form of the source. Use it when the thread is quiet, and the size of the thread tells more than an old reply. State `channel`.
  - Form `empty`: the link "See thread", and below it the note "There are no messages in this thread yet." This is a form of the source. Use it when the thread has no replies. State `thread-empty`.
- The whole card opens the thread, and the link is its one control. The quote above a reply is text in the sketch. In a product, a click on it can show the earlier message.
- The handle is the kit part `sketch-split-handle`. It sits on the line and is 16 px wide, so it covers no control of the channel or of the panel.
- The window has one bell, in the bar of the panel. The source has a second bell in the bar of the channel, for the notifications of the channel. The sketch leaves it out. The 2 bells had the same icon and different jobs, and the notifications of a channel are not a part of this record.
- The bell of the panel sets the notifications of this one thread. The help page of the source names 3 choices: all messages, only mentions, and no messages. State `notify-menu` draws them as a plain box that names `notifications/follow-menu`.
- The more menu has the 2 actions of a person who is not a moderator. The help page of the source names more actions: lock and delete for a moderator, and join and leave. A product adds them if it has moderators or thread members.
- The source names an archived thread a closed thread. The sketch says "archived", because the state `thread-closed` and the close button are about the panel.
- An archived thread with replies has a card of the form `count`. This is the designer's choice. In the pictures, the card of this form is 5 days old, and the help page gives 3 days as the usual time before a thread closes.
- The drag of the phone layout is a proposal from the owner's question. The source pictures show no phone. The drag only takes the thread away. The Back button and the drag show the same channel, so keep the two. The button is the way for a keyboard and for a screen reader.
- A drag that opens a thread from the channel is not in the record. A channel has many threads, so such a drag has no clear target. A tap on a card opens a thread.
- A tap keeps its transitions: the thread shows in 150 ms, and Back shows the channel at once. A person opens a thread many times in a day, so a move of 520 ms for each tap is too slow. Only the release of a drag has the move, because the thread must go on from the position of the finger.
- The record leaves out these states:
  - A reply while the product sends it: the reply shows in the panel at once, as in `reply-sent`. Only a send that fails changes the page.
  - A reply from a different person in an open thread: the reply shows at the foot of the panel, as the reply of `reply-sent` does.
  - The start of a new thread from a message: this is a different job, with a form for the thread name. The pictures do not show it, and it needs its own record.
  - The list of the threads of a channel: this is a different part. The pictures show only its button.
  - A deleted thread: the help page of the source says that the thread goes completely. Then its card goes also, and no state stays.
- The send buttons are the designer's choice. The pictures show no send button, because the source sends on Enter. A sketch needs a control that a person can see and click.
- The search of the channel is an icon button in the sketch. The source has a search field in the bar of the channel.
- A message in the channel field is not a part of this record, so its send button shows a note.
- The source has parts that only that app needs: a rail of workspaces at the left of the navigation, a badge on the name of an automated account, players for spoken messages, link previews, a tint on a reply that names the person, a mark on an edited message, a pin button, and buttons for gifts, pictures and apps in the message fields. The sketch leaves them out. `chat/voice-message` draws a spoken message.
- The names, the messages, the channel and the threads are sample content. The sketch keeps no name, message, avatar, logo or colour of the source. The pictures of the owner are private and are not committed.
