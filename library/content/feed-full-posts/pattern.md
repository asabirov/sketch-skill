# Feed of full posts

Captured: 2026-11-20, recorded by an agent.
Best for: a product where groups post updates, and a member keeps up with them in one stream. The member reads each post in full and answers without leaving the stream.
Row opens: in place. The row already shows the full post, and a click on the row body does nothing. The comment button opens a comment field of one line under the row. The row menu opens the page of the post.
Related: `content/feed-short-posts` has short posts with a row of counts, and its row opens the page of the post. `content/feed-topic-cards` has a card with a title for each post, and its card opens the post in a pane. `content/comment-thread` draws the comments.

## States

- `feed`: one column in the middle of the page. At its top, the title with a new update button at the right. Then the updates, under date labels with a rule. The first row is the pinned update. It has a pin mark and the word "Pinned" above its title. Each row has the group title with a more button at the right. Under the title, one line has the small avatar and the name of the author, and the age. The title, the author and the age are plain text, and none of them is a link. Then the text. A row with a picture shows a plain box under the text, and a row with a video shows a plain box with a play button and the length of the video. A long text shows its first 3 lines and a "Show more" button. Then a comment button, the reactions and an add reaction button. A reaction is a chip with an emoji and a count, and no word. The name of the emoji is the label of the chip. The comment button of a row with comments shows their count as words, the form `words`. Opens on: the feed link of the product.
- `feed-number`: the same feed. The comment button shows the count as a number, the form `number`. The source shows no such form. Opens on: no action. The product chooses one form.
- `feed-faces`: the same feed. The comment button shows the small avatars of the members who commented, and the count as words, the form `faces`. The source shows no such form. Opens on: no action. The product chooses one form.
- `text-open`: the long row shows its full text with its list, and a "Show less" button. The rows below move down. The source shows no such state. Opens on: a click on "Show more".
- `comments-open`: a comment field of one line stands under the row, and the rows below move down. A Cancel button and a Comment button stand next to the field, and the avatar of the member stands before it. The field has the focus, and it grows with its text. The Comment button is off until the field has text. Opens on: a click on the comment button.
- `comment-sent`: the same row. The comment button shows the count of 1. The comment thread stands between the row and the field. The thread is the pattern `content/comment-thread`, so the sketch draws a plain box with that name. Opens on: a click on the Comment button.
- `reaction-picker`: a popover next to the add reaction button, with a grid of 8 emoji. It has no search field and no word. Opens on: a click on the add reaction button.
- `reaction-added`: the chosen emoji is a pressed chip with a count, between the comment button and the add reaction button. A click on the chip removes the reaction. Opens on: a choice in the picker.
- `post`: the page of one post. A back link to the feed, then the title with a more button, the author and the age, the full text, the reaction chips and an add reaction button. Under them, the comment thread with its comment field, as a plain box with the name `content/comment-thread`. Opens on: the item "Open the post" of the row menu.
- `row-menu`: a menu with 7 items in 2 groups, each with an icon and a short name. The first group acts on the update: open its page, open it in its group, copy the link, copy it as text. The second group acts on the feed: pin the update to the top, unfollow the group, and "Why do I see this?". Space parts the groups. Opens on: a click on the more button of a row.
- `compose`: a dialog over the dimmed page. Its first line has a group chooser and a close button. Under it, a text field with no frame takes the width of the dialog. The last line has an attach button at the left and a post button at the right. Opens on: a click on the new update button.
- `empty`: a drawing, a heading, one sentence, a new update button and a link to the documentation. Opens on: a feed with no updates.

- The page of a post is the place for a link to one post and for a long discussion. The member does not need it to read or to answer, because the row holds both.
